What is Qetero?

Qetero is a simple place to request an appointment and to accept or decline one. It works for clinics, tutors, barbers or anyone who meets people by appointment.

How it works

  1. A client opens the Book page and sends a request.
  2. The provider checks the Schedule for free times.
  3. The provider uses the Respond page to accept or decline.
  4. The client receives the answer by email.

Who is it for?

  • Clients who want to book a time
  • Providers who want to accept or decline requests
  • Small businesses without a booking system
